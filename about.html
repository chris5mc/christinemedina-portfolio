About Christine

Christine Medina is a graphic designer, art director and freelance professional based in Toronto, Canada. With over 15 years of experience in creating print, digital, and social media designs for in-house organizations, Christine's passion lies in exceeding client expectations by crafting thoughtful design solutions that resonate with target audiences and meet business needs.
